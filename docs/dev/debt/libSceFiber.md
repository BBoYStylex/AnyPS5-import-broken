### Functional

- [Fiber guard page](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceFiber/Export.cpp) (libSceFiber) - `APS5_FIBER_GUARD=1` is a host debugging aid with no console counterpart and is implemented on Windows only. On Linux the variable stops the title at its first `sceFiberInitialize` with a not implemented error: a guest unmap of arena memory there maps the range again as a `PROT_NONE` reservation that cannot be told apart from the guard, and the Linux mapping and protection paths in libkernel do not go through one registry that could drop or keep a guard. On Windows `_sceFiberInitializeImpl` makes the lowest 4 KiB of a context `PAGE_NOACCESS` and `sceFiberFinalize` restores the protection the page had; a context that does not start on a 4 KiB page or is smaller than 8 KiB gets no guard and one line on stderr per process. A stack overflow into the guard ends the process with `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` at the faulting instruction but without the crash report, because Windows writes the exception frame below the stack pointer, on the guard page; a debugger stops at that instruction. A write into the guard made while the stack pointer is still above it is reported as usual. Any other read or write of the guarded page by the title or a host call faults as well; a GPU import of a range that contains the guard has not been tested. A context whose fiber is freed without `sceFiberFinalize` keeps its guard until the range is unmapped. Each fiber owns its guard through a ticket: fibers that share a context share the guard, and the last finalize removes it. With the context size check on, the fill skips the guarded page. A guest unmap or mapping over the guarded page, inside the guest arena or outside it, drops the guard, the next finalize of each owner is accepted once, and any other release of an unknown guard throws; the tickets of dropped guards are kept until their owners are finalized, so fibers that are never finalized after an unmap keep a ticket each for the life of the process. Every guest protection change of committed memory, inside the guest arena or outside it (the title image), goes through the registry, keeps the guard and becomes the protection the last finalize restores. A protection change by host code that calls `VirtualProtect` directly, such as the restore of guest thread stack pages at thread exit or `APS5_WATCH_WRITE`, is found when another fiber initializes on the guarded context or at the last finalize, which then throw instead of sharing the guard or restoring the protection recorded before; such a change that leaves the page `PAGE_NOACCESS` is not found, and the last finalize restores the recorded protection
